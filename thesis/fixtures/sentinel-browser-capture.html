Public guidance · August 2026

Policy update brief

Two changes take effect this autumn. This brief summarises what research teams need to know before the new guidance comes into force.

What is changing

Read the two summaries below for the scope and review requirements.

Effective 1 September 2026
DATA-14
Data management

Research data retention

Routine project logs will be kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically. Aggregated statistics may be retained once they no longer identify a project or contributor.

Approved guidance Review due September 2027
PUB-08
Publication

Publication review

Public summaries can be released after a project-owner check. Working drafts and unpublished findings remain internal until the responsible team approves publication.

Approved guidance Review due September 2027
