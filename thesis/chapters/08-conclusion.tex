\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

Browser agents must interpret web content to act, yet that content can also
redirect them. This dissertation asked whether independent runtime enforcement
can reduce such violations while preserving useful task completion.
\systemname\ separates planning from authority: BrowserUse and the model choose
actions, while observation handling and privileged execution are governed
outside the model.

Sentinel contributes an executor-side architecture that mediates live action
context through one policy, fail-closed outcomes, and paired audit; a
post-verified observation boundary coupled to narrow approval, direct takeover,
and fresh-capture incidents; and a two-environment evaluation connecting
controlled AgentDojo mechanism evidence to replicated browser-native WASP
evidence. A Chrome extension presents the server-owned authority states and
their redacted history.

The empirical answer is positive within that boundary. Across 629 attacked
AgentDojo tasks per condition, attacker success changed from 206 to 0 cases for
Qwen 3.5 4B, from 131 to 1 for 9B, and from 203 to 0 for 27B; utility and secure
utility increased in every enforce row. In the three-seed 27B WASP experiment,
full enforcement changed action-audited terminal success from 17/252 to 0/252
and attacker-directed execution from 42/252 to 0/252. Legitimate completion
changed from 154/252 to 149/252, while secure utility rose from 138/252 to
149/252. The ablation explains the mechanism: enforcement without sanitisation
still left attacker-directed execution in 15/252 episodes, whereas the full
system removed those proposals at the same aggregate utility and confirmed all
740 sanitisation attempts as removals.

This is a bounded systems contribution: it does not establish visual-channel
robustness, universal detection, deployment-scale isolation, or human
effectiveness. These boundaries motivate held-out adaptive evaluation, visual
mediation, a tested task-authority contract, and participant research.

The broader conclusion is architectural: under the tested conditions, useful
planning did not require unchecked authority. Sentinel governed what reached
the planner and what left it while preserving useful autonomy.
