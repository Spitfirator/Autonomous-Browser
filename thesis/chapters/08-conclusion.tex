\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

Browser agents must interpret web content to act, yet the same content can
redirect them. This dissertation asked whether independent runtime enforcement
can reduce prompt-injection-driven violations while retaining useful task
performance. \systemname\ separates planning from authority: the browser agent
chooses actions, while observation handling and privileged execution remain
governed outside the model.

Sentinel's executor boundary applies one policy to live context, fails closed
when context or authority is missing, and records paired outcomes. Its
observation boundary removes attributable hostile content, verifies
reconstructed input, and withholds unresolved
observations, while narrow approval and direct human steps preserve operator
authority. The Chrome extension is the browser-facing control plane: it shows
redacted server-owned decisions and relays scoped operator responses, while
authority remains in the runtime. The evaluation links controlled AgentDojo
evidence to replicated browser-native WASP results.

The evidence gives a positive but bounded answer. Across three matched
AgentDojo comparisons, the complete governed configuration reduced attacker
success from 20.8--32.8\% to 0--0.2\%, and utility rose for every model. WASP's
27B utility result was more mixed. Across three seeds, full enforcement
reduced audited terminal attacker success from 17/252 to zero and
attacker-directed execution from 42/252 to zero. Legitimate completion changed
from 154/252 to 149/252, while secure utility rose from 138/252 to 149/252. At
unchanged utility, policy-only/full adds mechanism evidence:
configured sanitisation reduced attacker-directed execution from 15/252 to zero.

The findings are limited to tested text channels, models, tasks, and the
prototype boundary; they do not establish universal detection, deployment
readiness, or human effectiveness. Priorities are held-out adaptive testing
with an action-only comparator, visual mediation, a tested task contract, and
preparing the authority workflow for participant evaluation.

Model safeguards are one layer; execution authority can remain outside the
model. In these evaluations, Sentinel reduced violations while retaining useful
autonomy.
