The live-representation correction reproduced BrowserUse's href-free,
split-line accessibility text for URL-format attacks. It changed 40 missed
WASP classifications and two already-detected score bands while leaving all
AgentDojo classifications and scores unchanged. The retained WASP false
positive was a legitimate GitLab profile page containing password, key, and
privacy language.

An action-gating replay explains why observation sanitisation remained the
primary browser control. WASP contains 20 cross-origin terminal goals and 64
same-origin form goals. Gating every tainted side effect produced a median of
eight requests per affected task (95th percentile 28, maximum 28) and exhausted
the four-request prompt budget on 35 of 50 replayed tasks. Requiring an
independent amplified sink reduced the median and 95th percentile to zero,
with a maximum of two and no budget exhaustion, while retaining the
cross-origin coverage. The replay did not change AgentDojo classifications.

The default approval budget is four requests per run. It is a configurable
lifecycle bound rather than a behavioural claim about operator attention.
Reusable grants remain run-local, expiring, use-capped, and bound to action,
origin, capabilities, taint, protected-data classes, target fingerprint, and
applicable parameter or content digests.
