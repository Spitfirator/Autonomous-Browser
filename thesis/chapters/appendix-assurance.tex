\label{app:assurance}

experiment. The table below separates the controls recorded in the frozen
campaigns from those supported only by implementation tests, preventing later
hardening from being credited to earlier results.

\section{Campaign provenance}
\label{sec:appendix-wasp-provenance}

Experimental settings matter because rows with different middleware or
provenance cannot be pooled. The AgentDojo table uses one thinking/32K profile
for all three models: a 32,768-token context, 4,096-token output cap,
temperature 0.6, top-$p$ 0.95, and seed 0. The final WASP experiment uses the
profile in \cref{tab:wasp-profile}. Its native-thinking stream is separated
from the schema-validated action output and is neither persisted nor treated as
an action. Exact immutable identifiers and source hashes remain in the
checked-in machine-readable result record.

The controller binds seed, condition, model, policy, generated inputs,
middleware, benchmark, and scorer identity to each checkpoint. Aggregation
fails on incomplete coverage or provenance drift, protecting the reported
denominators from partial or mixed-provenance cells. The final curated record
is
\nolinkurl{sentinel/evals/wasp/results/wasp-qwen35-27b-multiseed-thinking8k-replication-v2.json}.
Earlier 9B development records and the single-seed 27B comparison remain
separate and unpooled. Finally, no WASP human-participant or oracle-participant
result is reported.
