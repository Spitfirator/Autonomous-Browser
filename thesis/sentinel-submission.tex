\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\begin{singlespace}

Browser agents turn model outputs into consequential actions across web
applications, but the pages they observe may also contain instructions supplied
by an attacker.
This creates an authority problem: the acting model must interpret untrusted
content while deciding whether that content should influence privileged
actions. This dissertation presents \systemname, an independent enforcement
layer between a browser agent and its executor. \systemname\ governs the exact
observation sent to the model, evaluates model-proposed actions immediately
before execution, and transfers control to the operator when policy requires
human authority. Its implementation combines attributable observation
sanitisation and re-detection, origin-scoped taint, deterministic policy and
data-flow rules, narrow approval, direct takeover for protected input, and a
redacted audit trail presented through a Chrome extension.

The evaluation connects controlled mechanism evidence to browser-native
execution. Across paired AgentDojo comparisons with 629 attacked tasks per
condition, enforcement changed native attacker success from 206 to 0 cases for
Qwen 3.5 4B, from 131 to 1 for 9B, and from 203 to 0 for 27B. Utility increased
in all three enforce conditions. In the primary matched WASP comparison,
post-verified sanitisation changed terminal attacker success from 5/84 to 0/84
and attacker-directed execution from 17/84 to 0/84, while utility changed from
47/84 to 46/84 and secure utility from 44/84 to 46/84. A separate 9B
development sequence explains the mechanism choice: model-facing warnings left
attacker success unchanged, sanitisation V1 exposed incomplete multi-line
removal, and V2 addressed that observed failure class.

The results show that, under the evaluated conditions, an independently
enforced observation and action boundary can reduce successful attacks and
attacker-serving execution without eliminating useful task completion. The
contribution is a bounded systems result rather than a universal defence:
Sentinel demonstrates how a probabilistic planner can remain useful without
being the sole authority for its own privileged effects.
\end{singlespace}
